Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains what information the LIVLINK app (the “App”) and its website (the “Website”) collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using the App, you agree to what is described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Name you enter in your profile.
  • Phone number you enter; it appears in your listings so others can contact you.
  • City and neighborhood linked to the listing.
  • Listing content: title, details, and listing type (delivery request or delivery offer).
  • Location: entirely optional. We only ask for it when you tap “Locate via GPS” while posting a delivery request. You can also type the shop address manually or leave it empty.
  • Anonymous identifier created automatically by the App (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) with no email and no password; it is used only to link your listings to your device and to allow only you to delete them.
  • App settings such as dark mode and account type (shop owner or rider), stored on your phone.

2. Why we use this information

  • To show your listing to users in your city and neighborhood.
  • To let others call you or message you on WhatsApp.
  • To let the rider find the shop when its location is provided.
  • To protect the App from abuse and manipulation.

3. Who can see your information

The listing you post is public. It is visible to all users of the App: your name or shop name, your phone number, the city, the neighborhood, and the details, as well as the shop location if you provided one. Do not put anything in a listing that you do not want others to see. On the Website, listings are visible to any visitor without signing in and may be indexed by search engines such as Google.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Reports and moderation. You can report any listing that is abusive, misleading, or duplicated. We store the report (the listing ID, your anonymous ID, the reason, and the date) for moderation purposes only, and it is not visible to any user. We may remove listings that receive several reports or that violate the usage rules.

4. Where information is stored

App settings and your profile data (name and phone) are stored on your phone. The listings you post (including name, phone number, city, neighborhood, details, and the shop location if you provided one) are stored with the cloud provider Google Firebase (Firestore database with anonymous sign-in), subject to Google’s policies. Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Abuse protection. The App may use Google Play Integrity, and the Website Google reCAPTCHA, to verify that requests come from a genuine app or browser rather than automated bots. On the Website, only the list of listings you reported is kept in your browser (localStorage) to hide them from you; we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Retention

  • Listings are deleted automatically 7 days after posting, and you can delete your listing at any time before that. Expired listings are permanently removed from the database by an automatic cleanup task.
  • Your profile data (name and phone) stays on your phone until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Reports are deleted automatically after 30 days at most.

6. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • view and correct your information from the “My account” page;
  • delete your listings from within the App;
  • request permanent deletion of your account and all your data by emailing us at the address below;
  • withdraw location permission at any time in your phone settings.

We follow the principles of Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data.

7. Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

8. Permissions the App requests

  • Location (precise or approximate): only to set the shop location when you ask for it.
  • Internet: to display and post listings.
